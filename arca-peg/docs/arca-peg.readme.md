# Arca Swiss Plate + Multiboard Peg

Adapter that combines an Arca Swiss dovetail plate with a [Multiboard](https://www.multiboard.io/) push-fit peg, enabling camera mounting to multiboard systems.

## What It Does

Gives Arca Swiss clamps something to clamp onto standard 25mm push-fit peg. on one side and an Arca Swiss plate on the other.

## Specifications

- **Arca Swiss Plate**: 38mm × 32mm × 5mm (standard dovetail)
- **Peg**: Uses [25mm Push-Fit Peg](https://thangs.com/designer/Multiboard/3d-model/25%20mm,%20Push-Fit%20Peg-974205) from Multiboard
- **Print Orientation**: Side printing for strength
- **Total Height**: ~31mm when assembled

## Printing

- **Orientation**: Print on the side (pre-rotated in file)
- **Infill**: 20%+ recommended for durability
- **Material**: PETG or PLA
